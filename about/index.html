About UnPredictMe

UnPredictMe is a playful, unpredictable prediction service built around one simple idea: give us your email and we’ll make a prediction about you.

The point is not certainty. UnPredictMe turns tiny visible patterns in an email address into an intentionally surprising guess. We call it unpredictable because the fun comes from not knowing exactly what the machine will say.

How it works

  1. Enter your email.
  2. The machine generates a playful prediction — instantly, in your browser.
  3. Tell us whether we got you.
  4. Come back another day for another prediction.

No long quiz, no birth chart, no account setup. Built by Annie Li, an MIT student building playful AI experiences.
