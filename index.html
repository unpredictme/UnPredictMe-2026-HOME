What is UnPredictMe?

UnPredictMe is a playful prediction experience built around one idea: give us your email and we’ll make an unpredictable guess about you.

It is not a personality test, a birth chart, or a claim that an email address reveals who you really are. UnPredictMe looks at simple visible patterns — the shape, length, punctuation, and numbers in an email address — then turns those tiny signals into a surprising prediction.

Why is it unpredictable?

The prediction is intentionally playful. You don’t know which reading the machine will make before you try it — and you can tell UnPredictMe whether the guess feels like you. Come back another day for another unpredictable prediction.

Questions, unpredicted.

What is UnPredictMe?

UnPredictMe is a playful prediction experience that makes an unpredictable, fun guess about you from one tiny clue: your email address. Give it your email and it sends you three things: a prediction, something you hate, and something you'll like.

Is UnPredictMe an app?

UnPredictMe is currently a web experience at unpredictme.com — no download needed. Open the site, enter your email, and the prediction machine does the rest.

How does UnPredictMe make predictions?

UnPredictMe looks at simple visible patterns in your email address — things like its shape, length, punctuation, and numbers — and turns those tiny signals into a surprising, playful prediction. It is entertainment, not a personality test.

Are UnPredictMe predictions accurate?

They are intentionally unpredictable and playful — the fun is seeing whether a guess feels oddly close. Predictions are not facts about you, and should not be treated as advice.

Does UnPredictMe store my email?

The prediction itself is generated instantly in your browser — your email never leaves your device to make the guess. If you ask to receive your prediction by email, only then is your address used to send it.

Is UnPredictMe free?

Yes — UnPredictMe is currently free.

Who makes UnPredictMe?

UnPredictMe is built by Annie Li, an MIT student building playful AI experiences.
